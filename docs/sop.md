# The SOP: first-off check of the B-pillar outer

This is the ONLY procedure the app uses: **12 steps**. (A longer 25-step Word document exists. Ignore it. Do not show its numbering anywhere.)

Part: B-pillar outer, hot stamped 22MnB5, about 1.6 mm thick, about 1,100 mm long, laser trimmed. Fixture: checking fixture FX-014, Laser cell 3. All names and numbers are placeholders.

The Quality Technician takes every measurement and enters it on a tablet that is NOT part of this app. The app follows the procedure only. It never measures, never judges a reading, never decides tolerance, never releases the cell.

## The 12 steps

### Step 1: Put on gloves and safety glasses
- Phase: Setup
- Spoken line: "Step 1 — Put on gloves and glasses."
- What to do: Put on cut-resistant gloves and safety glasses before touching the part.
- What correct looks like: Gloves and glasses worn before the part is touched.
- Common mistake: Starting without them.
- Safety or rule: Trimmed hot stamped edges are sharp.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 2: Clean the pads and pins
- Phase: Setup
- Spoken line: "Step 2 — Clean the pads and pins."
- What to do: Wipe the pads and pins. No swarf, dross or coating dust.
- What correct looks like: Pads and pins wiped and clear.
- Common mistake: Loading the part on a dirty fixture.
- Safety or rule: Dirt on pads or pins causes false readings.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera sees this step partly
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 3: Lower the part onto pads P1 to P3
- Phase: Seating
- Spoken line: "Step 3 — Lower the part onto the pads."
- What to do: Lower the part onto pads P1, P2 and P3 first.
- What correct looks like: The part rests on all three pads before it touches the pins.
- Common mistake: Engaging the pins before the pads.
- Safety or rule: none
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 4: Round pin, then slot pin, no force
- Phase: Seating
- Spoken line: "Step 4 — Round pin first. No force."
- What to do: Slide the part until the round pin enters RPS-1, then the slot pin enters RPS-2.
- What correct looks like: Pins enter in that order with no force.
- Common mistake: Slot pin first, or pushing the part into place.
- Safety or rule: Never force the part. Forcing bends it and creates false deviations.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera sees this step partly
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 5: Close clamps lightly, C1 to C6
- Phase: Clamp
- Spoken line: "Step 5 — Close clamps in order, C1 to C6."
- What to do: Close clamps C1 to C6 lightly, one at a time.
- What correct looks like: Clamps close in order, one at a time. The part already sits on the pads and pins.
- Common mistake: Wrong order, or two clamps at once.
- Safety or rule: Never use a clamp to pull the part into place. Release and reseat it.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 6: Close clamps fully, same order
- Phase: Clamp
- Spoken line: "Step 6 — Close clamps fully, same order."
- What to do: Close the clamps fully, in the same order, C1 to C6.
- What correct looks like: Clamps close in order, one at a time.
- Common mistake: Wrong order, or a clamp pulling the part down.
- Safety or rule: Never use a clamp to pull the part into place.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

### Step 7: Check holes H1 to H14 with check pins
- Phase: Measure
- Spoken line: "Step 7 — Check the holes, H1 to H14."
- What to do: Try a check pin at every hole, H1 to H14, in order. Enter each reading on the tablet.
- What correct looks like: A check pin tried at every hole, in order.
- Common mistake: A hole is skipped.
- Safety or rule: Readings are entered on the tablet, not here.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: 14 points: holes H1 to H14

### Step 8: Check slots and notches
- Phase: Measure
- Spoken line: "Step 8 — Check slots and notches."
- What to do: Check slots S1 to S4 and notches N1 to N4 with the gauge or pin.
- What correct looks like: A gauge or pin applied at each feature.
- Common mistake: A feature is skipped.
- Safety or rule: Readings are entered on the tablet, not here.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: 8 points: slots S1 to S4, notches N1 to N4

### Step 9: Check trim edges T1 to T14
- Phase: Measure
- Spoken line: "Step 9 — Check trim edges T1 to T14."
- What to do: Place the flush pin or contour gauge at every point, T1 to T14.
- What correct looks like: A gauge at every point along each edge.
- Common mistake: A point is skipped.
- Safety or rule: Readings are entered on the tablet, not here.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: 14 points: trim edges T1 to T14

### Step 10: Check flange gaps F1 to F4
- Phase: Measure
- Spoken line: "Step 10 — Check flange gaps F1 to F4."
- What to do: Use the taper gauge at each of the four flange points, F1 to F4.
- What correct looks like: A gauge at each of the four points.
- Common mistake: A point is skipped.
- Safety or rule: Readings are entered on the tablet, not here.
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: 4 points: flange gaps F1 to F4

### Step 11: Look over the whole part
- Phase: Measure
- Spoken line: "Step 11 — Look over the whole part."
- What to do: Look over the whole part: trim edges, surface and marking (V1 to V5).
- What correct looks like: The whole trim, surface and marking looked over.
- Common mistake: A quick glance, one side only.
- Safety or rule: none
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera sees this step partly
- Check points in this step: 5 points: visual V1 to V5

### Step 12: Release clamps, C6 to C1
- Phase: Release
- Spoken line: "Step 12 — Release clamps, C6 to C1."
- What to do: Release the clamps in reverse order, C6 down to C1. Remove, rack and tag the part.
- What correct looks like: C6 down to C1, then the part racked and tagged.
- Common mistake: Wrong order, or the part left untagged.
- Safety or rule: none
- Camera visibility (assumed): Camera can see this step
- Check points in this step: none (single confirmation)

## Check points
50 in total: 5 seating points (A1 to A5, confirmed during steps 3 and 4), 14 holes, 8 slots and notches, 14 trim edge points, 4 flange points, 5 visual points. Only features are confirmed in the app, never values.

## Clamp order (steps 5 and 6)
Clamps C1, C2 and C3 first (over the pads), then C4 (near the round pin), C5 (near the slot pin), C6 (middle). Release in reverse: C6 down to C1. Never use a clamp to pull the part into place. Release and reseat.

## Scoring
Sequence conformance = steps done correctly on the first attempt divided by 12. Example: one deviation fixed on the retry gives 11 of 12, which is 92%. Two gives 10 of 12, which is 83%.
